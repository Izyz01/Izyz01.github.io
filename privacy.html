Privacy policy

Cosmo · last updated 4 October 2026

Cosmo is a private assistant used by one person, its owner, on their own computer. It has no other users. This page explains what it does with the Google account data they allow it to use.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: reads events, and adds or changes events they ask for.
  • Gmail: reads mail and writes drafts. It cannot send mail.
  • Google Drive: reads files (read-only). Google Sheets: reads and writes sheets they name.
  • YouTube: read-only access to the owner's own channels' details, analytics and estimated revenue. It cannot upload, edit, comment or change anything.

How it uses the data

Only to answer the owner's own requests and give them reminders and summaries. It is never sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

To answer a request, the relevant part of the data may be sent to the AI service answering it (Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's ChatGPT), under the owner's own accounts with them.

Where it is kept

Sign-in tokens and any saved results stay on the owner's computer, readable only by Cosmo. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can remove Cosmo's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which Cosmo can no longer read anything from that account.

Cosmo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
